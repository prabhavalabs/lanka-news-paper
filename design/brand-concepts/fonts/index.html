Thulawa: choose fonts and tagline

Pick a Sinhala font for the name තුලාව, a Sinhala font for the tagline, an English font for THULAWA NEWS, and a tagline. The round seal, the cover and the lockup on the right all update live. Click a weight chip to change thickness. When you're done, press Copy and paste it back.

1 · Sinhala name font: තුලාව

Used for the big name on the cover, lockups, and the ribbon on the seal (if you turn the ribbon on).

2 · Sinhala tagline font

Used for the tagline on the seal ring and under the name. Readability matters most at small sizes.

3 · English font: THULAWA NEWS

Used on the top of the seal ring and the English lines. Serif capitals suit a seal; sans fonts feel more modern.

4 · Tagline

Research notes: the current tagline පුවතේ සෑම පැත්තක්ම is nearly a word-for-word translation of Ground News's slogan, "See every side of every news story". Newsfirst (Sirasa) already uses "We report, you decide", so "you decide" lines are avoided. Top pick marks my recommendations.
