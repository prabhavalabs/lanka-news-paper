Data Deletion Instructions

Effective 18 October 2026

Facebook data

The Thulawa News Meta app is used only to publish posts to our own Facebook Page. It does not offer Facebook Login to the public, and it does not collect or store data about people who view or interact with our posts. So there is normally no Facebook data about you in our systems to delete.

If you have connected the app to your Facebook account and want to remove it:

  • On Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
  • Find Thulawa News and select Remove.

Comments you wrote on our Facebook posts are stored by Facebook. You can delete them yourself on Facebook at any time.

Other data

To delete anything else we hold about you, such as a problem report or your email briefing subscription, send a message to our Facebook Page, Thulawa News with the words “Delete my data”. Include the email address or details you gave us so we can find it. We will delete the data and confirm within 30 days.

See also our Privacy Policy.
