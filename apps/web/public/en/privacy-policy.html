Thulawa News

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Thulawa News (තුලාව) is a Sinhala news service based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. We bring together reports published by Sri Lankan news outlets at thulawanews.com and share news on our Facebook Page. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, and the choices you have.

Reading the website

  • You do not need an account to read thulawanews.com.
  • The public website sets no cookies and uses no advertising, tracking or analytics services.
  • Our web server records standard technical details of each request, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. We use these records only to keep the service secure and working, and we delete them within 15 days.
  • Some story photos and outlet logos load directly from the original publishers' websites. Those publishers may receive your IP address and browser details when your browser loads them.

Reporting a problem

If you use “Report a problem” on a story, we store your message and, only if you give one, your contact details. Our editors use them to review the report and, where needed, to reply to you. We keep a report for as long as we need it to handle the matter, and we delete it sooner if you ask.

Email briefing

If you ask to receive our email briefing, we store your email address and name to send it. Emails are delivered through our email provider, Resend. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

Facebook Page

  • We publish posts to our own Facebook Page through Loomcast, a publishing service, which receives only the post's image and caption. We do not offer Facebook Login, and neither we nor Loomcast collect or store information about people who view, like, comment on or share our posts.
  • Your activity on Facebook, including comments on our posts and messages to our Page, is handled by Meta under the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • For deleting data related to our Facebook Page, see our data deletion instructions.

Automated processing

We use automated tools, including AI services, to group, summarise and analyse news articles that outlets have already published. This processing concerns public news content. We do not use it to profile readers.

Where data is stored and who can access it

Our servers are hosted by Hetzner Online in Germany. Only the Thulawa News team and the service providers named in this policy can access personal information, and only to run the service. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it. These rights include those under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022. We will reply within 30 days.

Children

Our service is meant for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

Contact

Send a message to our Facebook Page, Thulawa News, or use “Report a problem” on any story on thulawanews.com.
